## What PCFM is

PC Football Manager: a community alternative to commercial football management games. Transparent, offline-first, deterministic. Built around the Doctrine of the Three Planes (Plane A = Ground Truth, Plane B = Observation, Plane C = Estimation). Technical documentation in English; ADRs in `docs/adr/`.
